# Annotation protocol

The current eight references are agent-authored synthetic expectations, not independent human validation. Every confirmatory question requires a human check of source table, metric definition, periods, units, all operands, arithmetic, cutoff availability and answerability. A second reviewer independently checks at least 25%, including all conflicts and unanswerable cases. Record reviewer identity or chosen anonymous ID, verdict, disagreements, adjudication and correction history. No acceptance is inferred from silence or an AI review.

An engineering pilot can ship with its actual review status. Human annotation and adequate independent groups remain gates for confirmatory performance claims. Existing v1 questions and every derivative remain development-only.
